## Learning New Tools : Metabase and Chatbot

Creating projects on Metabase and Chatbot was a whole new experience for me because we have never worked with these types of tools before. At the beginning understanding platform itself became very difficult because of it's tools and features, I faced lot of challenges while learning how to use them properly. In this process, I learned lot of things.

Since we had very less time, I was worried about the content a lot. I felt I took more time in learning this new features than working on things I need to put in the project. It happened in  both the projects. In Metabase dashboard, It was very challenging to understand the data and to come up with the things which gives proper information. I didn't even know about data which existed in the dashboard. So, I learned about deadly gases and why it matters and all. Same with chatbot too. In chatbot, I took little less time than I took for metabase. But problem was in connecting the all the possible questions which one can think of (About university) and responses which make my chatbot user friendly and interactive. 
In the process of learning and solving the challenges, I learned lot of things like patience, learning skill, problem solving and adaptibility to new things. Even though it was difficult to do it in less amout of time, we learned a lot. And being exposed to new experience brought so much joys too.
