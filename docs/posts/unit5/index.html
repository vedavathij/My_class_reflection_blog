Reflection On Group Presentation

One of the presentation that I found very intresting was the project about women in Computer Science. I liked this presentation because the group explained very well more than that I really liked this topic. As a girl in CS major, I have also faced lot of challenges in my home just to get into this field. When they mentioned about the challenges women’s are facing in cs field, I felt many of them were very familiar to my situation. They discussed lot of challenges like gender bias, lack of confidence, unequal opportunities etc. The presentation was informative and also made the audience think more deeply about the issues.

I also believed that that women in CS often face difficulties that are sometimes ignored or understimated. The discussion about self confidence and self effieciency are the factors which can strongly affect learning, participation, and career growth. I thought only social barriers were the problems for women to show their talents and skills which is why there is very less women’s achievement around the world. I was very shocked to know about womens like ‘Ada Lovelace’ and ‘Grace Hopper’, who have done lot of achievements but many of us don’t even know about it which shows how the world is so men dominant. The presentation made me realise that how these issues impacting society till now. We think that the generation has changed and everyone has equal rights and laws and all. But we don’t know how, even now also, people are facing all these issues. I apprec`iate how the group used researchand example to support their points. It helped make the presentation more convincing and connected the topic to real=life situation. Overall, I felt the topic was very relevent and important because inclusivity and equal opportunities are necessary in the technology field.
